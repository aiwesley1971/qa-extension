Tips and Precautions
📝
  • Don't be alarmed with 'debugging' alert on top. This extension utilizes some of Chrome debugging features.
  • Due to the nature of crawling, the results are limited and not 100% accurate — they are affected by different ways of PDP creation.
  • This extension is a simple tool to assist PDP QA work by quickly identifying potential mistakes in PDPs.
  • In 'List' view, 'Selector' row to highlight the corresponding element on the page. Note: not all element types produce a visible highlight.
